Our Story

Born from a passion for the African wilderness

A Legacy of Adventure

Founded in 2010 by native Kenyans with a deep reverence for the land, Jahari Safaris began with a single Land Cruiser and a vision: to share the raw beauty of Africa without compromising on luxury.

Today, we are a premier safari outfitter, recognized for our sustainable practices, world-class guides, and unyielding commitment to delivering bespoke, life-changing experiences.

"We don't just take you to the wild; we bring the spirit of the wild into your heart."

Our Mission

To provide unparalleled, authentic safari experiences that connect travelers with nature while supporting local communities and conservation.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and sought-after luxury safari brand in East Africa, setting the standard for sustainable tourism.

Our Values

Integrity, Passion, Respect for Nature, Excellence in Service, and Cultural Authenticity.

Commitment to Conservation

At Jahari Safaris, we believe that the beauty of Africa must be protected for future generations. That is why 5% of all booking revenue goes directly into local wildlife conservancies and anti-poaching units.

By traveling with us, you aren't just taking a vacation—you are actively contributing to the survival of endangered species like the Black Rhino and the African Elephant.

  • Partnered with Ol Pejeta Conservancy
  • Zero-plastic policy on all safaris
  • Empowering Maasai and Samburu communities
